How it Works

Step 1

The process begins when the user enters their basic information such as education, interests, and skills related to technology or creative fields.
The user may also select the field in which they want career guidance such as Artificial Intelligence, Development, or Designing and Animation.
This step helps the system understand the user’s background and current knowledge level.
It also identifies the user’s interests and preferred career direction.

Step 2

After receiving the user's information, the system processes the data using AI-based logic and analysis techniques.
The platform analyzes the entered skills, interests, and education details carefully.
It compares this information with different career categories available in the system.
The system evaluates multiple possibilities such as AI careers, Development roles, and creative fields like Designing and Animation.
This analysis helps the system determine which career path is most suitable for the user.

Step 3

After completing the analysis, the system generates personalized career recommendations for the user.
The platform suggests different job roles and career paths based on the user's skills and interests.
Each category provides specific recommendations such as AI Engineer, Web Developer, App Developer, or Graphic Designer.
The system may also highlight important skills that the user should learn to grow in the selected field.
